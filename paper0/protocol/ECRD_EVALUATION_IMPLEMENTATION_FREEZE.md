# ECRD evaluation implementation freeze

Date frozen: 2026-08-20

Status: prospective supplement to
`paper0/protocol/ECRD_MODEL_DEVELOPMENT_PROTOCOL.md`, frozen before any ECRD
engineering-smoke or scientific forecast result was inspected.

This document makes the reductions in Section 8 of the model-development
protocol executable. It does not change an acceptance threshold, architecture,
data interval, or training rule. Simulation 85606 remains unopened and is not
authorized by this document.

## 1. Immutable evaluation population

Every scientific score uses the 126 one-step targets `[498,624)` from 85604
and an independently generated 32-member ensemble. The three chronological
blocks are exactly:

- V00: `[498,540)`;
- V01: `[540,582)`;
- V02: `[582,624)`.

All scores are first calculated independently for model seeds 1701, 1702, and
1703. A window, voxel, Fourier coefficient, or ensemble member is never called
an independent physical shot. Target truth may be opened only after the
forecast file has been closed and SHA-256 verified.

## 2. Inherited metric implementations

The evaluator reuses the frozen B2 implementations for:

- gauge-aware field RMSE, MAE, bias, variance, fair CRPS, exact finite-ensemble
  coverage, and corrected spread--skill;
- expected-member directional and toroidal power;
- member-wise complex cross-spectra, phase, and coherence;
- the geometry-aware transport operator, with model-z 88 periodically
  resampled to native-z 81;
- member-wise nonlinear local and integrated transport before reduction.

The potential is gauge fixed only for field-error and spectral comparisons.
The unmodified physical potential is used by the transport operator. The
eligible spatial union and authoritative separatrix masks are inherited
unchanged. The stored mode mapping is `n=5k` because `zperiod=5`.

Physics-derived quantities remain evaluation-only and never enter training,
checkpoint selection, early stopping, or post-hoc calibration.

## 3. Three-seed reductions

Field fair CRPS is first reduced over eligible cells and fields for one seed,
then averaged with equal weight over the three model seeds. Field
spread--skill for one field is recomputed after pooling its member-variance
sum and ensemble-mean squared-error sum over seeds and targets. Its calibration
error is `abs(ln(spread_skill_ratio))`; the reported overall value is the
median across the five fields.

Expected-member spectral and complex cross-spectral sufficient quantities are
averaged equally over seeds before ratios, coherence, and phase errors are
formed. Material field-power checks use only the bands marked material by the
training-only materiality artifact. A material power check passes when its
expected-member power ratio lies in the inherited interval `[0.75,1.30]`.
The spectral-retention error is the median `abs(ln(power_ratio))` over material
field/band checks.

For the Ne--phi cross field, the overall and per-block comparison uses stored
`k=1..7` (`n=5..35`):

- complex cross-spectrum error is
  `sum_k |S_forecast(k)-S_truth(k)| / sum_k |S_truth(k)|`;
- coherence error is the truth-cross-amplitude-weighted mean absolute
  difference between forecast and truth coherence;
- cross-phase error is the same weighting of the absolute wrapped phase
  difference, reported in degrees.

An overall 10% cross-field improvement means candidate error is at most 0.90
times B5 error. “Improves in two blocks” means strict numerical improvement in
at least two of V00, V01, and V02. Phase may be at most two degrees worse than
B5 overall.

## 4. Spatial and integrated transport covariance

For each transport quantity, each target produces a local exact-separatrix
vector with shape `[16,81]`. The full spatial comparison is the relative
Frobenius error between:

`(33/32) mean_t Cov_member(q_t)`

and

`mean_t [(q_truth,t-q_mean,t)(q_truth,t-q_mean,t)^T]`.

The second expression is intentionally an uncentered forecast-error second
moment, so deterministic bias is not erased. The Frobenius ratio is estimated
with one shared bank of 64 normalized Rademacher probes generated by NumPy
PCG64 seed 85604350. The same probes are used for every arm, seed, quantity,
and block. Seed aggregation is the arithmetic mean of the three independently
estimated relative errors. The primary spatial-covariance statistic is the
median of those four seed-aggregated errors.

Integrated transport spread--skill is recomputed for each quantity after
pooling the per-target ensemble variance and squared ensemble-mean error over
all three seeds. Its calibration error is `abs(ln(ratio))`; the family value is
the median across particle, electron internal energy, ion internal energy, and
total internal energy transport. “Increase by 0.10 toward one” requires a B5
ratio below one, a candidate increase of at least 0.10, and a strict reduction
in absolute distance to one.

Local transport spread--skill is recomputed from the stored local diagonal
member-variance and squared-error sums pooled equally over seeds. No local
ratio may exceed 1.25 and at least three of four must lie in `[0.80,1.25]`.

## 5. Robustness and paired uncertainty

Seed-level robustness counts a seed only when both its overall eligible-union
fair CRPS and its median integrated-transport `abs(ln(ratio))` improve over the
same-seed B5 result. At least two of three seeds must satisfy both directions.

The paired moving-block bootstrap uses noncircular blocks of 12 consecutive
frames, 2,000 replicates, NumPy PCG64 seed 85604351, sampling with replacement
from every valid block start and truncating concatenated blocks to 126 frames.
The same indices are applied to candidate and same-seed B5 sufficient
statistics. Each replicate recomputes the three-seed fair-CRPS improvement and
the three-seed integrated-transport calibration-error improvement. The lower
2.5th percentile of both improvement distributions must be strictly positive.
These intervals are conditional on simulation 85604.

## 6. Executable acceptance logic

An ECRD arm is eligible only if all seven frozen families pass:

1. fair CRPS improves by at least 2% in every block, and overall median field
   calibration error improves by at least 10%;
2. material spectral error is no more than 1.05 times B5 and the inherited
   material-power pass count does not decrease;
3. Ne--phi complex-spectrum and coherence errors improve by at least 10%
   overall and strictly improve in at least two blocks, while phase worsens by
   no more than two degrees;
4. median spatial-covariance error improves by at least 15%, with strict
   improvement for at least three transport quantities;
5. median integrated-transport calibration error improves by at least 20%, at
   least three ratios move by 0.10 toward one, and the median ratio is at least
   0.60;
6. all local ratios are at most 1.25 and at least three lie in `[0.80,1.25]`;
7. at least two same-seed comparisons improve in both primary directions and
   both paired-bootstrap lower bounds are positive.

If both ECRD and ECRD-History pass, ECRD is selected unless ECRD-History's
median integrated calibration error is at most 0.90 times ECRD's. B5-Context
is reported as the conditioning ablation but cannot by itself authorize the
held-out release. No scalar score substitutes for the seven-family gate.

## 7. Scope lock

This freeze authorizes only 85604 forecast scoring and model selection. It does
not authorize opening 85606, assimilation, diagnostic ranking, steering,
production-scale architecture expansion, or result-dependent threshold edits.
